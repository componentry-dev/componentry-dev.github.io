The properties panel

A block's panel averaged 2,829px, nearly four screens, and the shared Advanced section was more than half of it on every block. Sections now fold, opening on the content and folding the settings; help sits behind a "?"; one list entry opens at a time; and a search box narrows the panel to the settings that match. This pins all of that, across every block in the library, so a new block or a new field cannot quietly bring the long panel back.
